Item Types & Colors
Categories are the color groups (e.g. Aluminum Cutting = green). Types are the actual values assigned to items (e.g. "flat end mill" lives under Aluminum Cutting). Label header colors come from whichever category a type belongs to. Add new categories — like "Cleaning Products" — and give them their own color and types.
