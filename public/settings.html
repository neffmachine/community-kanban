AI Screenshot Import
Anthropic API Key
Optional. Lets you paste a product screenshot into Add Item and have the description, part number, vendor and price filled in for you.

You use your own key and pay for your own usage. There is no shared key and nothing is billed to anyone else. Without a key the rest of the app works exactly the same — this one feature is simply off. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

The key is read from this server's environment and is never saved into your inventory data, so it can't end up in a backup or an export.
Checking… The key is stored as a Cloudflare secret, not in the app. To set it, run:
wrangler pages secret put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
